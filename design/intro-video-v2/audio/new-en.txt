This is PodiumCall. It shows who is most likely to finish in the top three at the next big athletics championship, using real results from World Athletics. Start on the Dashboard. Each card is the athlete the model rates highest in one event. Track and Field show the world's top 20 in any event. Rank them By points, for the best performance this season, or by Model rating, for who the model thinks will reach the podium. Choose a name to see that athlete's best marks, their races this year and the titles they've won. Press the little i next to any number to see what it means. Follow the championship that's on now, and the ones still to come. Each gets its own page, with the model's podium chances, event by event. Results shows what the model called before each championship, and what really happened. Nothing is changed afterwards.
