No fabricated data, anywhere in the pipeline.

Six commitments that separate a model from a guess.

01

Real results, not hand-typed

Every mark is scraped directly from World Athletics results pages — no manual entry, no rounding of our own.

02

Validated honestly

Walk-forward tested across the 2023–2025 seasons, scoring only the real Final field. The 71.9% is that hit rate, not a training-set number.

03

Injury and withdrawal aware

Athletes ruled out are removed with a source link, so a projection never counts someone who will not line up.

04

Wind and venue adjusted

Marks are read in context — altitude, wind and indoor vs outdoor are all part of the feature set, not ignored.

05

Head-to-head history

Who has actually beaten whom, and how recently, feeds every projected field — real race results, not seed times.

06

Live all season

Re-scraped and re-scored as results land, right up to the last meeting before the Final.
