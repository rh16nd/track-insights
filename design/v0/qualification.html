How to read it

The margin, not the medal

Points come from finishing position at each Diamond League meeting a runner contested — eight for a win, scaling down from there. Akani Simbine holds the eighth and final place on exactly 17 points, level with the cut. Emmanuel Eseme, the model's pick to win the Final, only scraped in seventh. Noah Lyles — a household name — sits ninth on 15, two points shy, and misses out entirely.
